The lightweight path

No training step. Same robotics concepts.

Ed-Zh/Basketball-Analytics — pose estimation and trigonometry, about 560 lines of Python. Each card: what the code does, then what robotics calls it.

mediapipe.Pose

33 body landmarks per frame, from a model someone else already trained.

↓
Perception

The camera is the sensor. Pixels become a structured state vector.

KNEE = (24, 26, 28)

Hip, knee and ankle become a thigh, a shin, and the angle between them.

↓
Kinematic chain

Rigid links joined at revolute joints — how a robot arm is described.

savgol_filter

Smooths every joint signal before anything gets differentiated.

↓
Sensor filtering

Differentiating a noisy measurement amplifies the noise. Filter first.

np.gradient

Joint angle over time becomes angular velocity, then acceleration.

↓
State estimation

Deriving the rates your sensor never measured directly.

alpha ∝ torque

Angular acceleration at a joint implies the force that caused it.

↓
Inverse dynamics

Working backwards from observed motion to the effort behind it.

argmax(alpha)

Peak timing per muscle, measured against the glute as reference.

↓
Closed-loop control

Measure, compare to a setpoint, emit one correction. Repeat.

Zero-training MVP

Pretrained pose estimation + trigonometry + coaching rules

Measure

Elbow angle, knee bend, torso lean, wrist height, shoulder-hip alignment.

Segment

Dip → rise → release → follow-through using wrist/hip movement over time.

Coach

Flag the frame, explain the issue, give one correction at a time.

angle = arccos( dot(a,b) / (|a| * |b|) ) if elbow_angle_at_release > threshold: feedback = "Elbow may be flaring at release."
